Primary mark

Quick rules

Do
  • Lead with legibility and hierarchy.
  • Use line art for interface icons.
  • Use compact mono labels for system details.
  • Make motion quiet and optional.
Avoid
  • Make orange the default surface colour.
  • Add rust, flames, rivets, or medieval ornament.
  • Use chrome effects on body copy.
  • Invent a second icon style.
