Why this layer is deferred to a follow-up

Breeze’s widgets/panel-background.svgz is a FrameSvg carrying four prefix families — "", mask-, shadow-, thick- — each with nine tile elements plus hint-*-margin / hint-*-inset markers (~50 named elements in one file, and button.svgz is larger again).
A FrameSvg element that is missing or misnamed renders as nothing — it does not fall back to the inherited theme — so an unverified hand-authored replacement risks a transparent panel on the ISO, which is strictly worse than today’s known-good Breeze inherit.
The two viable routes — author the full set from scratch, or vendor and patch Breeze’s SVGs (which makes foundry-kde-theme a partial fork of Breeze artwork, with its own copyright and drift burden) — are a design call above this pass’s remit, and either needs a live Plasma session to check. Shapes and measurements above are the spec for whichever route wins.
